The 768px tablet proof retains the two-column spread. Desktop interaction confirmed that the Guide route renders its comparison table, focuses its heading and has source links. These checks support the observed states, not a claim of universal accessibility or measured animation smoothness. The original artwork is an interpretive plate, not a plot of model capabilities.
