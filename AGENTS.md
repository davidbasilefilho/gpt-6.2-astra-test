## Review lessons to keep

- **Visual direction:** this is an illustrated field atlas, not a dashboard. Follow the landing’s square corners, two typefaces and original engraved art. Avoid ornamental chapter labels, decorative microcopy, oversized gaps, and generic cards. Build a useful composition before adding words. The comfortable light paper and rich plum spread should read as different stocks; grain belongs _behind_ content and needs per-theme calibration on the body as well as panels. Compare dark/light crops at 1:1; neither an invisible texture nor TV static is acceptable.
- **Icons and controls:** use the shared Tabler primitives/sprite for conventional UI marks; keep the custom sunrise brand, observatory, celestial body drawings and constellations. Use actual SVG arrows, not uncommon text glyphs. Keep a visible Theme label and recognizable current-state icon, a correctly centered rotating chevron, and ample 44px hit targets even at 360px. Interactive labels are nonselectable; reading copy and editable fields remain selectable. A visible label must occur in the accessible name. Replace native UI title tooltips with the Base UI primitive, not the document title.
- **Movement:** use Motion entries to explain the layout; coordinate them with the router’s native View Transition and capture the colophon with the article. Never flash a footer over an empty lazy route. Avoid double fades, scrolling jumps or hidden content on back/forward. Controls, tooltips, menus, popovers and collapsibles need coherent transitions in _both_ directions. Use the tokens, spring physical affordances rather than reading copy, and hold the panel in the DOM until exit settles. CSS minification can turn 260ms into .26s; test computed production timing. Inspect intermediate frames of the actual optimized bundle.
- **Celestial plate:** autonomous movement must work without a mouse. Keep the 13 illustrated stars, but make each of the two constellations one target, alongside Sol and the two original orbiters as Luna and Terra; don't add a mode switch, pause button or figure label. Use one focus stop and a safe hover area per constellation, with keyboard/touch access, accurate moving hitboxes and a safe path into the popover. Use dashed orbits. Gently re-anchor on group changes and scroll. Weight real Sol direction more than pointer position (75:25) for the subtle 3D perspective on the _inner_ visual surface; not the geometry used for hit testing. Respect reduced motion and keep everything usable when motion is off.
- **Evidence:** distinguish the pre-release tester reports from the launch changes, and include both Tibo’s explanation and Theo’s qualification. Keep formal thresholds and selected evaluation failure rates distinct from production prevalence. Date estimates and label the model, effort, harness and provenance. Never transplant old subscription caps or past-model scores to 6.2; task completion depends on token mix, rejected attempts, model-specific limits and quota per attempt. Separate API-equivalent use, activated API credits, and provider costs.
- **Release checks:** compare user references, the previous version and the compiled candidate in the cloud browser. Cover desktop/tablet/phone (including ≤390px), all routes, pointer/keyboard/touch paths, light/dark/system and reduced motion. Exercise actual route links/Back, cold direct entry, rapid reversal, scroll during movement and footer timing. Check horizontal overflow without indiscriminately clipping overlays; 100vw includes the scrollbar, a container query width need not. Capture scroll/overlay transitions on video or a clearly labeled timestamped sequence. Fix concrete regressions, freeze, and let requested independent reviewers recheck the _same_ build before shipping. Keep QA artifacts out of production.
- **Every motion has an origin.** Reveal a menu or tooltip from its trigger-facing edge, recomputing from final collision-aware placement; close toward that same origin. Anchor route snapshots to the activating pointer or focused link; history has a neutral reading-edge fallback. Reveal cards from their viewport entry edge, draw data bars from the measurement baseline, expand accordions away from their header and rotate icons about their visible pivots. Keep color/opacity-only feedback spatially neutral. Verify flips, scroll, interruption and the no-motion path.

### Animation direction checklist

Use all twelve principles deliberately across the system; do not add twelve effects to every widget. **Staging:** group highlight and retain a clear reading order. **Anticipation:** prepare actions with hover/focus, never input delay. **Timing** and **slow in/out:** use bounded changing orbital speed and distinct short enter/exit curves. **Arcs:** orbit along the depicted ellipses; rotate icons about visible pivots. **Straight-ahead/pose-to-pose:** derive ambient art from elapsed time; use interruptible start/open/end poses for controls. **Follow-through/overlap:** let inner marks settle without blocking focus. **Secondary action:** animate the rays and constellation luminance gently behind the principal motion. **Squash/stretch:** deform decorative marks, not hitboxes or text. **Exaggeration:** limit emphasis to a small response at activation. **Solid drawing:** separate position, hit-testing and 3D visual layers. **Appeal:** retain the legible etched silhouette in both themes. See DESIGN.md for actual bounds and no-motion behavior.
